# US-10: Campus-Hub Team 5

## User story

As a student, I want to get an email every morning listing everything due this week, in order by due date and time, so that I don't forget anything even if I don't open the app.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Priority | Medium |
| Sprint # | TBD |
| Story Points | To be agreed in Planning Poker |

## Acceptance criteria

- The email is sent once a day in the morning (around 7:00 a.m.).
- It lists my items that are not completed and are due in the next 7 days, soonest first.
- Each item shows its title, class, and due date and time.
- It only has my items, not anyone else's.
- If nothing is due that week, no email is sent.
- The email has a link that opens the app.
